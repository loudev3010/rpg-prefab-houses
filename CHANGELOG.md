# Changelog

## 1.8.0 (Forge 1.20.1): Magic Room stations for Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks and Forbidden & Arcanus

**What's new**

The Magic Room can now come with real working stations from two magic mods. Each has its own switch, so you can have one, both or neither.

- **Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks:** the Inscription Table (where you bind spells into spell books), the Scroll Forge and the Arcane Anvil, set along the walls with room to use them.
- **Forbidden & Arcanus:** a ready-to-use Hephaestus Forge, built on its full darkstone floor exactly as the mod expects, already lit. Right-click it and it works.

**How to turn them on**

1. Install Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks and/or Forbidden & Arcanus. They are separate mods and aren't included.
2. Open a house in the catalogue and press **Customise**.
3. In the **Mods** section, switch on **Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks** and/or **Forbidden & Arcanus: Hephaestus Forge**.

The house needs its Magic Room switched on and the Varied architecture. If a mod isn't installed, its switch is greyed out and says why. The note under each switch shows the Magic Room's size and what it costs.

**The Magic Room gets bigger only when it needs to**

With either switch on, the Magic Room is made larger to fit the stations and still leave plenty of free floor for your own things. It is at least 11 x 13 with the Iron's stations, 11 x 17 with the forge and 11 x 19 with both. With both switches off, every house is built exactly as before.

**What it costs**

In survival, the blocks are added to the house's price:

- Iron's Spells: 1 Inscription Table, 1 Scroll Forge, 1 Arcane Anvil.
- Hephaestus Forge: 48 Polished Darkstone, 9 Arcane Chiseled Polished Darkstone, 4 Chiseled Arcane Polished Darkstone, 1 Smithing Table and 1 Mundabitur Dust (the forge is what the dust makes of the table).

The placement panel shows anything you're missing. If something is missing, the house isn't placed and nothing is taken. Creative players and exempt operators build it for free.

**Undo**

You can't undo a house while the Scroll Forge or the Hephaestus Forge still holds items, or the forge holds essences. Empty them first. Nothing is ever deleted.

**Good to know**

- The forge's floor works whichever way the house faces. The places for ritual pedestals are left free.
- The Wizard Tower's round tower is too small for the forge at Small and Medium size, and so is the Treehouse cottage at Small. Customise tells you and suggests a larger size.
- The Builder's Hammer doesn't add the stations or the forge.
- Without a mod, saved designs that used it open with its switch off.

**Before you update (servers)**

- The server and every player must update to 1.8.0 together. 1.7.x players can't join a 1.8.0 server, and 1.8.0 players can't join a 1.7.x server.
- To build these stations on a server, install the mod on the server and on every player's game.
- Existing designs, codes and presets build exactly the same houses.

**Versions**

Minecraft 1.20.1 with Forge 47.1.0 or newer. Tested with Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks 1.20.1-3.16.3 (which itself needs Forge 47.4.0 or newer) and Forbidden & Arcanus 1.20.1-2.2.6. NeoForge 1.21.1 has its own 1.8.0.

## 1.7.0 (Forge 1.20.1): Storage vault for Tom's Simple Storage

**What's new**

Houses can now come with a ready-made storage network from Tom's Simple Storage. Switch it on and the house's Storage Room is built with 20 chests, already linked to a Storage Terminal and a Crafting Terminal. You can reach everything from one place as soon as the house is placed.

**How to turn it on**

1. Install Tom's Simple Storage. It's a separate mod and isn't included.
2. Open a house in the catalogue and press **Customise**.
3. In the new **Mods** section, switch on **Tom's Simple Storage vault**.

The house needs its Storage Room switched on and the Varied architecture. If Tom's Simple Storage isn't installed, the switch is greyed out and says why.

**What it costs**

In survival, the vault adds 20 Chests, 1 Inventory Connector, 1 Storage Terminal and 1 Crafting Terminal to the house's price. The placement panel shows anything you're missing. If something is missing, the house isn't placed and nothing is taken. Creative players and exempt operators build it for free.

**Undo**

You can't undo a house while its vault still holds items, including anything left in the Crafting Terminal's grid. Empty it first. Your items are never deleted.

**Good to know**

- Only the Storage Room changes. The rest of the house is built exactly as before.
- A few small size combinations don't leave room for the vault. Customise tells you and suggests a larger house or room size.
- The Builder's Hammer can't add a vault.
- Without Tom's Simple Storage, saved designs that used the vault open with it switched off.
- Fixed: long setting names in Customise now wrap instead of being cut off.

**Before you update (servers)**

- The server and every player must update to 1.7.0 together. 1.6.x players can't join a 1.7.0 server, and 1.7.0 players can't join a 1.6.x server.
- To build vaults on a server, install Tom's Simple Storage on the server and on every player's game.
- Existing designs, codes and presets build exactly the same houses.

**Versions**

Minecraft 1.20.1 with Forge 47.1.0 or newer. Tested with Tom's Simple Storage 1.20-1.7.1. NeoForge 1.21.1 has its own 1.7.0.

## 1.6.7 (Forge 1.20.1)

**See what is in the way**
- When a house can't be placed because something stands in the way (a chest, a furnace, another mod's machine, a
  player's build), the message now names the nearest few blocks and their coordinates, so you can find and move
  them. Before, it only said how many there were. The same goes for the mine's warnings, Undo's refusals and land
  that is protected (it names the first protected column).

**Safer Undo**
- A chest someone put down where a house was still being built is now left standing, items and all. Before, the
  build spilled its items on the ground and Undo then brought the chest back full.
- Undo is refused if a block of the house was replaced (not just broken), or if an item in one of its chests was
  swapped for one that only differs by its name or enchantments. The refusal says which block and where.
- Storage and machines from other mods inside the house now count as yours, like your own chests: take them out
  before you undo.
- Blocks you have built inside the house since it went in are left standing by Undo, and it tells you how many.
- When the mine's recovery chests are full, the leftover items lie beside them. Undo is now refused once any of them
  has been picked up, so they can't be kept as well as the rock Undo puts back.
- Storage from other mods that the mine cuts through is emptied into the recovery chests.

**If a build fails**
- A house that fails part way through building now puts the land back as it was before the price is given back.
- If a house fails to build while its owner is logged out, they get the price back when they return (unless the
  server restarted in between). If it builds while they are away, they can still undo it once they are back.

**Land claims and the world border**
- Claims, spawn protection and the world border are now checked for every column a house covers, including the land
  blended round it and its paths, not once per chunk. Claim mods that claim less than a whole chunk are respected.
- The Builder's Hammer's rule that a house built without its enchanting room can't be given a Magic room is now
  kept by the server too, not only by its screen.

**Smoother on servers**
- Placement requests are limited to a few a second per player, and the quick checks (spectator, catalogue, distance)
  run before a design is generated. The deep mine is dug a little at a time instead of in one go.
- The Builder's Hammer checks distance and whether a house is still being built nearby before it builds.

**Fixes**
- House previews no longer show the wrong textures after pressing F3+T or changing resource packs.
- A damaged saved-designs file no longer loses every design after the damaged one; an unreadable file is kept as
  `rpgprefabhouses-presets.json.bad`.
- The seed box no longer accepts a number larger than a seed can be (it used a different seed than it showed).
- The placement panel shows the price of the house that will actually be placed when the land splits it into levels.
- In the catalogue, C and Enter do nothing when the Customise and Place buttons are greyed out.
- Loading a saved design or a random one no longer keeps basement and mine choices from the previous design.
- A vine on the land round a lot is no longer laid back beside a garden wall it can't hang on.
- The mod's description says 20 genres.

Existing design codes build exactly the same houses. 1.6.6 and 1.6.7 can play together; update both to get every fix.
This update is for Minecraft 1.20.1 with Forge; the NeoForge 1.21.1 version has its own 1.6.7.

## 1.6.7 (NeoForge 1.21.1)

**See what is in the way**
- When a house can't be placed because a container, a functional block or part of a build is in the way, the message
  now names one of those blocks and its coordinates, for example "Would overwrite 1 containers or functional blocks
  (one is Chest at 120 64 -30)". The placement ghost also draws a red box round every block in the way.

**Safer Undo**
- Undo no longer removes blocks you put down after the house went in, such as a block in one of its rooms or on the
  land around it. It leaves them standing and tells you how many it left.
- Undo is refused if a block of the house was swapped for a different one, or if a chest's contents changed, now
  including enchantments and other item data. Natural changes (grass, farmland, copper weathering, ice, growing plants)
  don't count.
- Storage blocks from other mods count as containers for Undo, like chests.

**Containers and building**
- A chest or other container put down where the house is still being built is left standing with its contents. Before,
  the house was built over it and its items were spilled.
- If building a house fails part way, what was built is taken down again and the price is paid back. If you have left
  the game, you get it when you next join.

**Deep mine**
- The mine is dug a little at a time, so digging a deep mine no longer causes a lag spike.
- Storage blocks from other mods in the mine's path are emptied into the recovery chests, or left standing if they
  can't be emptied safely.
- If the recovery chests overflow and you pick up any of the items dropped in the bottom chamber, Undo is refused
  until they are all back.

**Land claims, spawn protection and the world border**
- These are now checked for every column under the house and its mine, not once per chunk, so a house can no longer
  reach a few blocks into spawn protection, past the world border or into a claim.
- The blended land, the paths, the retaining walls and the tree clean-up around the lot also stay out of land you
  can't build on.

**Builder's Hammer**
- Spectators can't extend houses, and you need to be within the placement distance of the house.
- An annex is no longer built onto a house that was undone, or onto one that is still being built or undone.
- Turning through the spots around a house is quicker.

**Servers**
- Placement requests have a short cooldown, and the quick checks run before a house is generated, so a modified
  client can't overload the server by repeating requests.
- A house is no longer placed for free if you leave the game while it is being prepared.

**Other fixes**
- House previews and the placement ghost show the right textures after you change resource packs.
- The price shown while placing matches what you pay on split-level houses. A wing stepped down with the land no
  longer counts as a basement.
- The seed box only accepts seeds a design code can keep. Before, a larger number silently became a different seed.
- The catalogue's C and Enter shortcuts do nothing while their buttons are greyed out.
- One damaged entry in your saved designs file no longer loses the designs after it. The file is backed up before
  it is next saved.
- The mod list description now says 20 genres.
- A test-only structure is no longer included in the mod.

**Compatibility**
- Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge 21.1.1 or newer (tested on 21.1.259). Install it on the server and on every client.
- Placed houses, saved designs and design codes from 1.6.6 work as before, and the same design code builds the same
  house.
- Tested on a dedicated server with Open Parties and Claims.

The Forge 1.20.1 version is not changed by this release.

## 1.6.6 (Forge 1.20.1 and NeoForge 1.21.1)

**Now on Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge**
- RPG Prefab Houses now runs on Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge, as well as on 1.20.1 with Forge. It's the same mod:
  the same 20 styles, the same Customise settings and the same Builder's Hammer, and a design code builds the same
  house on both.
- The Forge 1.20.1 version stays available and gets the same updates. This is an extra version, not a replacement.
- Needs NeoForge 21.1.1 or newer (tested on 21.1.259). Install it on the server and on every client.
- Houses you placed in a 1.20.1 world are still recognised when the world is opened in 1.21.1, so you can still extend
  them with the Builder's Hammer. Back up your world before moving it to a new Minecraft version.
- Decorated pots and bells now turn with the house, like every other block (in 1.20.1 they kept their facing).
- Tested with Sodium and Iris shaders.

**Land claims respected**
- With some claim mods (such as Open Parties and Claims) a house could be placed inside another player's claim.
  Placing a house, or extending one with the Builder's Hammer, now also asks the claim mod whether you may build
  there, and is refused if any claimed chunk under the house or its mine says no. Claims from mods that already
  worked (such as FTB Chunks) and spawn protection are respected as before.

**Undo gives the grass back**
- Grass, mycelium and nylium under a house turn to dirt while it stands. Undo now puts them back as they were.

**Adventure mode**
- Adventure-mode players follow Minecraft's adventure rules: placing or extending a house needs a Prefab Catalogue
  or Builder's Hammer in hand whose "Can place on" list names the ground there. Before, adventure players could
  place houses anywhere.

The land-claim, Undo and adventure changes are in both versions. Existing design codes build exactly the same
houses. On Forge 1.20.1 only the server needs 1.6.6: 1.6.5 clients can still join.

## 1.6.5

**Tower stairs fixed**
- The stairs that wind up the inside of a tower could not be walked up: at every turn you met the next step from
  the side and had to jump. This affected the towers you add with the Builder's Hammer, and the towers inside
  houses (Wizard's Tower and the other round-towered styles). Every step now faces the way you walk on to it, so
  you can walk up and down without jumping. The house's other staircases are unchanged.

**New: Underground On / Off**
- A new **Underground** switch at the top of the Underground settings in Customise. Off builds the house wholly
  above ground: no basement, no rooms in the basement, no mine and no stairs going down. On brings back the
  basement and mine you had.
- With it off, the basement, mine and underground style settings and the Mine room are locked until you switch it
  back on.
- Cottages, the Swamp Manor, the Hillside Burrow and the Treehouse always keep their underground, because their
  rooms are down there.
- A small house may not fit every room above ground; the screen then says it is too small, so pick a larger size
  or more floors.
- Builder's Hammer extensions never have an underground of their own.

Existing design codes build exactly the same houses. Client and server both need 1.6.5.

## 1.6.4

**Compatible with shaders (Oculus, Embeddium)**
- With a shader pack on, the house you are placing showed as a solid black shape instead of the see-through green
  (or red, when it can't be placed) preview. It now shows as a see-through, tinted preview with shaders too, lit by
  the shader pack.
- With shaders, the placement outlines could also show up a second time, floating in the sky, and the preview cast a
  shadow on itself. Both are gone.
- The Builder's Hammer extension preview had the same problem and is fixed too.
- Without shaders, everything looks exactly as before. Embeddium on its own with shaders off was not affected.

Only the client changed: a 1.6.4 client can join a 1.6.3 server and the other way round. Houses build exactly as in
1.6.3.

## 1.6.3

**Fixed: gaps in upper-floor windows**
- On houses whose upper floor juts out over the wall below (Rustic Townhouse, Storybook Manor, Medieval Manor, Swamp
  Manor, Alchemist House and Village Homestead), part of a window could be pushed out with the jutting wall while the
  rest stayed behind. The glass panes either side of the step met only at a corner, and panes cannot join that way, so
  the window showed a gap.
- A window now moves out with the wall as a whole, together with the wall on each side of it, or stays where it is.
  Bay windows, window frames, shutters and every window size are unchanged.

Houses in those six styles can come out slightly different around their upper windows, so saved designs in them are
marked as made with an older version. Every other style and the Original architecture build exactly as before.
Servers and clients must both run 1.6.3.

## 1.6.2

**New: Terrain setting**
- Customise > Terrain: **Terrain Adapted** lays the house into the land as in 1.6.1. **Flat Platform** puts it on a
  level lot and blends the land around it to meet the lot, the way Original houses are placed.
- On a Flat Platform, trees the lot cuts through are taken down whole, along with any leaves and vines left hanging.
  The ghost marks the trees that will go.
- Original architecture always stands on a flat platform, so the row is greyed out for it.

**New: Path Size**
- Customise > Path Size: **Expanded** keeps the paths as before: a main path three blocks wide, and every path carried
  on over the land to a nearby road.
- **Compact** makes the main path only as wide as the front door, and only that path carries on a short way past the
  lot.

**New: room switches**
- The Storage Room can now be switched off. The Village Homestead and Coastal Estate keep it, since they are built
  around their barn and warehouse. A house without a storage room has no starter chests.
- **All On / All Off** above the room list switches every room that can be switched.

**Improved: houses laid into the land**
- The land around a Terrain Adapted house is shifted to meet the lot instead of being flattened, so it keeps its shape.
  Where the lot already meets the land, nothing moves. Around 10% less land is reshaped on the test sites.
- Past a three-block yard round the house, open lawn that sits at the land's own height keeps the land's own ground
  and plants. In woods and wild land the forest floor now runs up to the yard, between the paths and garden features.

Existing designs and design codes keep the houses they always built. Servers and clients must both run 1.6.2.

## 1.6.1

**New: Varied architecture**
- New designs use **Varied** architecture (Customise > Architecture). The seed now changes every style's shape, and
  large houses are built from several volumes instead of one block.
- Varied wall windows come in pairs with blank bays, set round a wider middle window. Dormers and side gables vary
  with the seed, and the yard has a working side and a garden side.
- Rooms that belong together sit side by side, and bedrooms keep away from noisy rooms.
- **Original** architecture builds every house exactly as before. Saved designs and design codes keep the house they
  always built.

**New: room decoration**
- Decoration can be **None**, **Selected Rooms** or **All Rooms**. You pick the rooms floor by floor in the Customise
  screen.

**New: houses laid into the land**
- A Varied house now sits on the land where you place it, instead of on a flat lot. The house stands on level ground,
  and the garden slopes into the surrounding land as grass. Stone walls appear only where the style has them
  (terraces for the Keep, Dwarven Hall and Greek Villa, plinths, podiums).
- Wings can step up or down a little with the slope. The Dwarven Hall sets itself into the rock.
- Hillside styles sit lower in the slope, and houses on stilts stand on posts.
- The front door gets a level landing and a path that leads out to open land. On slopes the path has steps, and over
  water it has a deck.
- Trees are kept unless they are in the way. A tree that is in the way is taken down whole, with nothing left
  floating. A narrow gap opens so you can see the front of the house as you walk up to it.
- The placement ghost shows the house as it will stand on the land. It marks the trees that will be cleared, and
  shows the path squares at their real height.
- If the land is too steep or would need too much digging, the ghost moves or turns the house to a nearby spot that
  fits and tells you. If there is no such spot, it says why the house can't go there.
- Servers can switch this off with the `fitTerrain` setting. Varied houses then go on a level lot as in 1.6.0.

**New: Mine and Magic Room can be switched off**
- The Mine and the Magic Room (enchanting room) are now ordinary on/off rooms in the room list. Both are on by
  default. With the Mine off there is no shaft, chamber or digging, and the Mine size is greyed out. With the Magic
  Room off the house is planned without it.
- The Builder's Hammer won't add a magic room to a house that was built without one.

**Fixes**
- Undo no longer refuses with "Part of the house has been broken" when a leaf from a tree next to the house decays.
- A one-block gap could be left where a very tall tower meets the house wall; it is now filled (Varied houses only).

Existing designs and design codes keep the houses they always built. Servers and clients must both run 1.6.1.

## 1.6.0

**New**
- **Every size choice now goes Small, Medium, Large, Huge, Massive**, and each size really builds bigger than the
  one before:
  - **Room Size**: Small, Medium and Huge are the old Compact, Spacious and Grand; **Large** (taller storeys, rooms a
    little bigger) and **Massive** (8-block storeys, wide halls and stairs, the biggest rooms) are new.
  - **Basement**: new **Massive** basement reaching out under the garden (it takes Large grounds or more). A Large
    basement in a house with only one wing now runs under the whole house instead of being the same as Medium.
  - **Mine**: new **Small** mine and a **Massive** one with a 13-block shaft, a 23 × 21 chamber 8 blocks high and two
    long side tunnels. Medium, Large and Huge are the old Standard, Large and Very Large.
  - **Grounds**: new **Massive** grounds with room for more garden features and trees.
  - **Builder's Hammer**: new **Huge** and **Massive** wings and towers, chosen with arrows like the room.
- The tooltip of every size setting tells you what the size you chose measures (storey height, corridor and stair
  width and the smallest bedroom; the mine's shaft, chamber and tunnels; the garden in front, behind and at the sides).
- **Tiny** is a form of its own (Classic / Offset / Cottage / Tiny).
- When one choice changes another (a Massive basement takes Large grounds, a cottage keeps two optional rooms), the
  screen says what changed.

**Fixes**
- Clicking Size could show the previous size again or stop changing after a few clicks; Back and Customise again
  kept the old size. Every click now shows and keeps its size.
- **Garden Props** placed nothing in most styles (the stone skirt round the walls was in the way); props now stand
  in front of it.
- Settings that changed nothing are gone or explained: the style's own wood, stone and roof show as *Style Default
  (Spruce)* instead of appearing twice; Balcony needs a Porch and two floors (it stands on the porch roof); the
  Desert Oasis and Mountain Keep porch is offered on their cottages, where it is built; the great hall of the Ranger
  Lodge and Nordic Longhouse is always there. When a porch, balcony, roof trim or props have no place in a layout,
  the warnings say so.
- **Colour** says what it does: it recolours the carpets, beds, banners and wool inside the house.
- Long tooltips wrap instead of running off the screen.
- Consistent wording across the screens and key hints that follow your key bindings.

Existing designs and design codes keep their sizes. A few build slightly differently: designs with Garden Props

## 1.4.0

**New**
- **Three new genres**, each with its own cottage: the **Gothic Manor** (tall stone ranges, steep roofs, a
  tower, pointed windows and buttresses), the **Dwarven Stone Hall** (a low stone hall dug into a rock face, with a
  carved portal, a forge and a dwarven underground) and the **Eastern Fantasy House** (a hall on a stone podium with
  wide curved roofs, courtyards at the bigger sizes, a lily pond with a bridge and a grotto underground). The rock
  face of the Dwarven hall can be switched off in the Customise screen.
- **Rooms in the basement**: a single-storey house keeps its big rooms in a cellar under the garden, so every genre
  builds at one floor. It is switched on for you when you choose 1 floor, and you can switch it off.
- **Every genre builds at its smallest size with one floor.** When the genre's own rooms do not all fit, the house
  leaves out the ones that do not and names each of them ("No space for the library at this size..."). If even its
  main rooms do not fit, the big rooms move to a cellar under the garden, and as a last resort the rooms get a size
  smaller; each of these says so. Rooms you tick yourself are never left out.
- Faster: houses generate about twice as fast, and placing a house costs the server much less each tick (each
  layer of a house now costs only its own blocks).

**Changes**
- **Windows keep solid wall between them and every door**, and every window sits in solid wall all round. An
  extension's new doorway walls up any window pane right beside it.

**Fixes**
- Designs that could not be built now build: the Massive single-storey fairy hut, narrow mountain keeps, shallow
  hillside burrows with big rooms, and desert oases without towers.
- Fixed rare crashes and flaws: overlapping longhouse annexes, floating flowers and lanterns, swamp water over a
  basement room, and steps or window sills you could climb onto but not off.

Because of the window change, **saved designs and design codes may build slightly different houses** than in
1.3: windows next to doors move along the wall or are left out. Saved presets show their "may look different" note.
Servers and clients must both run 1.4.0.

## 1.3.2

**New**
- New server setting `exemptOperators` (in the placement section). It's on by default, so nothing changes:
  operators, and the owner of a single-player world with cheats on, place houses for free like creative players.
  Modpacks that want everyone to play by the survival rules can turn it off: operators then pay the placement price
  and need the catalogue (and the hammer for extensions). Creative players always build for free.

## 1.3.1

**Fixes**
- The 3D preview now shows overlay and connected textures correctly in packs with texture mods (such as Continuity
  and Fusion). Before, parts of some blocks showed as black and white patches in the preview.

## 1.3.0

**New**
- **Size** in the Customise screen: Small, Medium, Large, Huge or Massive. Sizes are relative to the style, from
  the smallest footprint its rooms fit in up to 64 blocks on the long side, in the style's own proportions. Picking
  one sets the width and depth, and the sliders below still fine-tune it. Catalogue cards show each house's size.
- **1 to 5 floors for every style.** Where a style doesn't usually go that high or that low, it adapts: the fairy
  hut keeps to three floors and its towers get taller, the longhouse keeps its two-storey hall and raises its end
  bays, a tall hillside burrow sits inside a bigger hill, and the tower styles keep their towers above the house
  when it has fewer floors.
- The tiny cottage is now the **Small cottage**: choose Cottage, then Small. Surprise me rolls Small cottages and
  unusual floor counts now and then.

**Fixes**
- The Desert Oasis with corner towers never built at some sizes (34-38 and 42-44 blocks). It now builds at every
  size.

Saved designs and design codes build exactly the same houses as before.

## 1.2.1

**New**

## 1.2.0

Designs saved with an older version may look slightly different when rebuilt.
